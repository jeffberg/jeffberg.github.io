colophon

materials

html and css, nothing else. one self-contained file per page, served as written. no framework, no bundler, no javascript, no fonts loaded over the wire.

type

cormorant garamond when your system has it, falling back through eb garamond, garamond, hoefler text, and times new roman. labels in your system’s ui sans.

palette

warm paper #f4efe6, ink #1a1614, with rose, sky, sage, lavender, and ochre drifting through the gradient field behind the poem.

motion & texture

a slow 32-second drift on the gradient and a 1.6-second rise on the words. an svg feTurbulence grain at low opacity. all of it nullified under prefers-reduced-motion.

visibility

intentionally invisible to search engines — robots.txt blocks every crawler, and every page carries noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet. found, when found at all, by being shared.

hosting

github pages, auto-deployed from main. ci validates html structure, required meta tags, and basic accessibility on every push. no analytics, no cookies, no third parties.
